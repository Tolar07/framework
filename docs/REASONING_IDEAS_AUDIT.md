## Order of work and first out-of-sample reads

Measured in `backtest/MARKET_CONSISTENCY_STUDY.md`, `BIAS_CHECKS_STUDY.md`, `RECORDS_STUDY.md` (records replayed per board, later half) and `REASONING_IDEAS_STUDY.md` (To-do #7 research session). CLV is near zero throughout (+0.2% board average), so nothing yet beats the close.

1. **Cross-market consistency** — built, recorded only. First out-of-sample read (5,067 games, later half from 17 Oct): sides the grid calls soft (gap > +1%, chosen on the earlier half) lose −6.0% vs −13.2% for the other side of the same lines, but inside the 1.20–2.00 band they match every side (−6.9% vs −6.8%, CLV −7.5% vs −7.7%). That is favourite-longshot composition, not an edge. **No case for stakes.** Keep recording; re-read once the live `xm` field has the last look and Matchbook closing to compare.
2. **Bias checks** — built, recorded only (`engine/bias_checks.py`, `single["bias"]` on every board single, both sides + `short_leg`). Walk-forward read (81,064 sides, 17–27 Oct): favourite-longshot is strong (1.01–1.20 −3.7%, 4.00+ −23.3%). Inside a band, the market × band lean separates in 1.50–1.75 (too long −5.2% vs too short −12.9%) and 2.00–2.50 (−5.4% vs −12.8%), but no cell is profitable. Board singles: legs under 1.30 −6.8% ± 2.6 (416) vs 1.30+ −1.2% ± 5.9 (100), not yet separable. **No case for stakes.** Public-team bias is **missing**: there is no public-money data source.
3. **Timing** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/timing.py`: hours to kick-off, after line-ups, SportyBet move, sharp gap for both sides, steam / reverse line move / fade). Measured so far by `REASONING_IDEAS_STUDY.md`: following Matchbook steam at SportyBet's last price −0.6% ± 13.9 (n 84), no edge. Past boards keep only their last write, so `RECORDS_STUDY.md` can't replay timing; it reads live rows as they build up.
4. **Context facts** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/context_facts.py`: squad value missing, team news, cup rotation, each with source / side / direction / size; rest, travel, motivation, weather, referee, new manager and derby listed as unknown because no source is wired). Later half: a fact against the pick −5.8% ± 3.4 (264) vs a fact for it −6.1% ± 3.4 (274). No separation.
5. **Exposure** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/exposure.py`: rank within the league, a 4-a-league cap, same match, acca same-league pairs with the measured +0.0073 pair covariance, nightly stake total). Later half: inside the cap −2.8% ± 2.7 (392) vs past it **−12.5% ± 4.4 (171)**, which agrees with `REASONING_IDEAS_STUDY.md` (−1.6% vs −13.1%). **This is the strongest result. It goes to the Architect as a proposal, not wired in.**
6. **Shadow Kelly** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/shadow_kelly.py`: full / market / shrunk Kelly, model-vs-sharp disagreement, the other side's edge). Later half: shrunk > 0 −4.8% ± 3.1 (329) vs 0 −7.1% ± 3.5 (234). Too close to separate.
7. **Ensemble** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/ensemble.py`: model vs Matchbook weight learned by log-loss on earlier boards only; recently w = 0.7 on 162 picks). Only 31 later-half singles carry a sharp price, so there is no read yet. Dixon-Coles, Elo and xG join once they are recorded on the single.
8. **Drift alarm** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/drift_alarm.py`: two-sided CUSUM per family, K 0.02 / H 2.0 from `REASONING_IDEAS_STUDY.md`, where half stake under the alarm gained +1.91% ± 1.15 a pick). In `RECORDS_STUDY.md` the alarm fires on most families, because the CUSUM never resets. Re-tune after a month of live rows before reading it.
9. **Price shop** — built, recorded only (`engine/records/price_shop.py`: Matchbook price after 2% commission, best book, SportyBet edge vs sharp, the other side's Matchbook price). Later half: Matchbook paid more on all 140 picks that had a sharp price; `REASONING_IDEAS_STUDY.md` reads price-shopping at the close at +11.6% ± 17.0 (77), too noisy to call.
