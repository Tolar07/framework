**The evidence that forced it** (`backtest/PRICE_BAND_STUDY.md`, recomputed from
`output/picks/picks_*.json` on every run, so it never goes stale):

- 332 graded board singles: stated 78.0%, landed 78.0% — calibration error 0.0
  points — at an average price of 1.242, which needs 80.5% to break even.
  Return −3.4% ± 2.8. **The prediction is sound. The price is not.**
- By band: 1.00–1.25 −1.2% ± 3.3 (207 picks); 1.25–1.30 −0.6% ± 5.4 (94);
  **1.30–1.50 −29.4% ± 12.3 (30 picks, landed 53.3% against a 75.2%
  break-even)**; 1.50–2.00 one graded pick.
- By tier: SAFE (model and market agree) −5.1% ± 3.2 over 262; MARKET
  (bookmaker only, no model) +3.5% ± 6.1 over 56; VALUE 2 picks.
- Over 272 legs priced against SportyBet's OWN closing price, mean CLV
  −0.043% ± 0.149 (item 37 of STATE.md): the board's entry price IS the closing
  line. A calibrated model betting at the close returns minus the margin.
- The board has been printing the same verdict daily and sending anyway:
  "Sharp check: 0 of 13 picks priced above the Betfair Exchange's fair odds
  (average −5.9% vs fair)".

**What changed:**

1. **Order 3 amended.** The band 1.20–2.00 and the ban above 2.00 are unchanged.
   The **"safest 1.50" target is withdrawn**: 1.30–1.50 is the board's worst
   band, not its safest. The board's footer no longer calls any price "safest"
   and points at `backtest/PRICE_BAND_STUDY.md` instead (`output/produce_bet.py`).
   `engine/slate.DEPLOY_ODDS_SAFE` is kept as the band's midpoint label only.
2. **Order 7 amended.** Deploy coverage and sharp-reference coverage are
   different things. The board's coverage is unchanged. The exchange prices only
   the **19 of our 32 SportyBet leagues** that have a football-data division
   code; the Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, FA Cup, Turkish
   Cup, UEFA Nations League and the new-leagues countries (Denmark, Poland,
   Croatia, Austria, Switzerland, Norway, Sweden) have none, so the parallel list
   produces nothing for them — never a guess (HR35).
3. **Order 37 amended.** Value is measured against the **sharp market**, not
   against our own fair odds. TABLE 3C keeps doing what it does, and the board
   says plainly what it is (our model disagreeing with SportyBet). Alongside it,
   a **parallel list** runs every day on the board's own target day
   (`engine/sharp_value.py`, `monitor/sharp_list.py`, `daily.yml`): take an
   outcome only when SportyBet's price beats the Betfair Exchange's margin-free
   price by ≥ 2%, inside 1.20–3.00, and never when it beats it by more than 25%
   (a stale line or a mismatched fixture — reported as suspect, not picked).
   **On that list orders 4 and 11 do not apply** (no ≥ 50% floor, no
   most-likely-outcome). **Orders 3, 4 and 11 remain in force for the board,
   unchanged** — the Architect's "let's keep the board as it is".
4. The parallel list never changes a board pick, an acca or a code. Own store
   (`data/sharp/sharp_<date>.json`), real SportyBet booking codes, graded by the
   same Flashscore grader, reported beside the board's own record in the same
   study. Most days it is short; some days it is empty, and an empty day reads
   `NO PICK — PENDING`.
