# European Competition Catalogue

Generated by **querying** API-Football, football-data.co.uk, TheSportsDB and The Odds API. Not written from memory — every row came from a live directory. Regenerate with `python generate_catalogue_doc.py`.

**756 competitions across 51 countries** — 453 leagues, 303 cups.

## Reading the coverage columns

A competition needs **all three** to produce a board row. Two of three is still NO DATA — PENDING.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **History** | `FD` = football-data.co.uk — the best source, because it also carries the opening/closing odds the CLV backtest needs. `APIF` = API-Football — broad coverage, but the free tier stops at **2024**, so anything sourced there is ~2 seasons stale and is flagged as such on the board. |
| **Fixtures** | Something to predict. Currently TheSportsDB, whose *shared test key* resolves only a handful of leagues — a personal key would widen this considerably. |
| **Odds** | An entry price. Required for HR30 (numerical MES) and HR46 (CLV logging). A competition out of season simply isn't listed, so a `—` here can become `yes` once it kicks off. |

> Coverage is checked against the mappings actually wired into the pipeline, and competition names are pinned to their home country — Scotland's Championship does not inherit the English Championship's sources.
